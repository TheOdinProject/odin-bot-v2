# Privacy Policy for Odin Bot

**Last updated:** October 11th, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, and sharing of personal data by Odin Bot, a Discord bot developed by [The Odin Project](https://www.theodinproject.com/).

## Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to Discord users who join and interact with [The Odin Project's Discord Server](https://discord.gg/fbFCkYabZB). Odin Bot is not used in other Discord servers.

Odin Bot operates with Discord. [Discord Inc. has its own privacy policy](https://discord.com/privacy) governing your Discord account and everything you do on the platform. The Odin Project and Odin Bot are not affiliated with Discord Inc. This policy only covers what Odin Bot does.

## Data We Collect

Odin Bot may process the following data provided by Discord:

- User ID
- Username
- Display name
- Role and permissions data
- Message content

Odin Bot cannot access personal account data such as your email, phone number, or password.

## How We Use Your Data

We use your data strictly in the following ways:

- Message content may be temporarily read for operations such as commands and awarding points, as well as moderation actions. We do not retain message content.
- User IDs and roles are read for commands and moderation. We retain user IDs for features such as our points system, bot automation, and staff rotation queues.
- Display names are used to provide more readable names for command output.

## Sharing Your Data

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, and we do not pass any data to AI products or services. However, we may disclose your data in response to legal process or a request from a law enforcement agency or regulatory authority.

## Data Retention

While some data may be processed temporarily (in-memory during Odin Bot operations), some data is stored where necessary. We only store data that is required for functionality, and we aim to minimize the data we collect and store where possible. Any data we collect and store is retained for as long as necessary to provide Odin Bot's functionality.

We will delete your data upon your request or when it is no longer needed.

We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no security measure is perfect, and we cannot guarantee the security of your data.

## Changelog

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make a material change, we will update the date at the top and log the change below. Your continued interaction within The Odin Project's Discord server and use of Odin Bot after we make changes to this policy indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.
